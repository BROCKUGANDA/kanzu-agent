# Emigaso gy'AML/CFT — Obutonde bw'Uganda

**Ensibuko:** Etteeka ly'Okuggya Ensimbi ez'Obusaasi (Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013, Uganda), Ebiragiro bya FIA, n'Omwongozo gwa FATF ku Eby'Obugagga bw'Abalimu Abatonotono.

## Ekitegeeza ky'Okuggya Ensimbi ez'Obusaasi

Okuggya ensimbi ez'obusaasi (money laundering) kitegeeza okukola ensimbi ezava mu bikolwa by'obusaasi zirabike nga ezaweebwa obwangu. Entegeka esinga okukolebwa eri:

1. **Okuteereka (Placement):** Ensimbi z'obusaasi zitwalirizibwa mu ngeri ezimanyika ez'obugagga — SACCOs, amabanki, oba mu lukalala lwa agent-banking.
2. **Okusengulula (Layering):** Ensimbi zizunguluzibwa mu makawunti amangi oba mu bikolwa okuziggya okuva ku nsibuko yazo.
3. **Okuggya (Integration):** Ensimbi ezisaasiddwa zikolebwa nga ezaweebwa obwangu era zikyusibwa omulimba ogwa nyonyi.

## Etteeka ly'Okuggya Ensimbi ez'Obusaasi (AMLA 2013) — Uganda

Etteeka ly'Uganda lya 2013 lyateekawo:

- **Enteekawo ya FIA (Financial Intelligence Authority):** FIA ye mamlaka y'enjawulo ey'obukakafu bw'ensimbi mu Uganda. Ekirwanye Okuggya Ensimbi ez'Obusaasi (Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013, §3).
- **Omugereka gw'Okuwanika:** Ebyenfuna by'akapooti aka UGX 20,000,000 (ensimbi ezimukwano) oba okusussa bigirimu bibi kiri mu bbanga ly'emisana 30 birina okuwanikibwa eri FIA.
- **Obukuubirirwa (Customer Due Diligence — CDD):** Ebibiina by'obugagga bina okumanya abapolisi baabyo, ensibuko y'ensimbi, n'okubeera kw'ebyenfuna okw'amangu.
- **Abakulembeze ab'Ekibiina (Politically Exposed Persons — PEPs):** Abakulembeze ab'ekibiina mu Uganda (abalekwa ba Gavumenti, abakiiko, RDC, n'abakiiko b'obutaka) baatwalibwa ng'ab'obucwezi obuwaggulu era baetaaga okumanya okulinganira.

## Omukwano gw'SACCO mu Uganda

Ebibiina by'ababbi (SACCOs) mu Uganda bikuubirirwa Akawunti ka Matteeka ga SACCO (UMRA — Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority). Emirundi egya UMRA:

- **Okuwanika kw'Ensimbi ez'Obucwezi:** SACCOs zina okuwanika ebyenfuna eby'obucwezi eri FIA n'UMRA.
- **Obukakafu bw'Omuntu (Know Your Customer — KYC):** Omupolisi buli omu alina okukirizibwa n'okuweebwa ddirisa ly'obukakafu nga bw'ateekateeka:
  - **Tier 1:** Akawunti w'obwereere (simplifiée) — ekyenfuna eky'omuwaggulu UGX 5,600,000 ku mwezi.
  - **Tier 2:** Obukakafu obugatta ebibonebwa — ekyenfuna eky'omuwagguju kiri waggulu.
- **Obubonero bw'Omugereka:** SACCOs erina okukozesa omugereka ogw'omu nju gwa UGX 28,000,000 (nkooye za UGX 2,800,000,000) okuwanika ebyenfuna ebisusaamu.

## MTN MoMo n'Airtel Money — Omukwano wa Agent-Banking

Uganda yalina omugereka gw'okuteeka agent-banking mu mwaka gwa 2017. Ebiyunga:

- **MTN Mobile Money (MTN MoMo)** n'**Airtel Money** bwe bibiina by'obugagga ebikulemesebwa nga by'obugagga bw'ekibiina era byetaaga okukuuma amateeka ag'AML.
- Ensimbi ezitumibwa oba ezirebwamu ku lukalala lwa agent-banking zitatekebwa kwonka mu mawulire g'ebyenfuna by'SACCO.
- Omugereka gw'obuŋŋaana kwa agent (agent concentration) — agent omu abeera n'ebikumi 80%+ by'obutunzi bw'omupolisi — kiyagalika okukebera.

## Ensimbi ez'Obucwezi — Mitindo Egyayitiddwa

Mitindo egyayitibwako okukulemesebwa mu Uganda:

| Omuwendo | Obuzibu |
|----------|---------|
| Buli ekyenfuna ky'omuwendo ogususaamu UGX 20M | Etaago lya CDD n'okuwanika |
| Okugabanya obutunzi paka wansi w'omugereka | Structuring (R01) |
| Okutuma okutuusa UGX 28M mu maawa 72 | Okuwanira omuvudde (R02) |
| Okukyusa akawunti eyali eriho n'ensimbi nnyingi | Okuddamu (R04) |
