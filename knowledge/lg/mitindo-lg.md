# Mitindo gy'Obucwezi — Obutonde bw'Uganda

**Ensibuko:** Omwongozo gwa FATF ku Eby'Obugagga bw'Abalimu Abatonotono (2013), Eddiini y'Okuggya Ensimbi ez'Obusaasi ya Uganda (AMLA 2013), Ebiragiro bya FIA ku Mitindo egy'Okuggya Ensimbi mu SACCOs.

## Mitindo Egyayitibwako mu Uganda

### 1. Okugabanya Obutunzi (Structuring / Smurfing) — R01

Omupolisi ateereka obutunzi obunene mu bikolwa bitono bitono, buli kimu wansi w'omugereka ogw'okuwanika:

- **Ekyokulabirako:** Obutunzi bwa UGX 33,600,000 bwateekebwa mu bikolwa 5 bya UGX 6,720,000 buli kimu, mu naku bbiri. Buli kimu kiri wansi w'omugereka gw'SACCO gwa UGX 28,000,000, naye waggulu wa UGX 33,600,000.
- **Okugalabika:** Bikolwa bingi by'omuwendo omukusu mu bbanga etonotono; ekyenfuna kya jumla kisusaamu omugereka.
- **Etteeka:** AMLA 2013 §18 (okugabanya obutunzi kuyambibwa okuwanira).

### 2. Okuwanira Omuvudde (Transaction Velocity Spike) — R02

Omuwendo gw'ebyenfuna by'omupolisi mu bbanga etonotono gususaamu emirundi 4+ gy'ekkomo lye.

- **Ekyokulabirako:** Omupolisi eyasula UGX 560,000 buli sabbiiti, alina omuwendo ogw'obuseera gwa UGX 4,480,000 mu sabbiiti emu.
- **Okugalabika:** Omuwendo ogwa bbanga ogususaamu emirundi mingi ekkomo lye lyasooka.

### 3. Okuzungulusa Ensimbi mu Makawunti Amangi (Multi-Account Cycling) — R09

Ensimbi zizunguluzibwa mu makawunti amangi ag'SACCO — entegeka eyayitibwako mu bibiina by'abavuzi ba boda-boda:

- **Ekyokulabirako:** Omukuumi w'ebibiina ateereka ensimbi mu makawunti 4 ag'omupolisi endala endala mu maawa 48, aziggyaayo era aziddamu kuteeka — omuwendo gwa waggulu UGX 14,000,000.
- **Okugalabika:** Ensimbi zizunguluzibwa mu makawunti amangi mu bbanga etonotono; SACCO ekozesebwa nga omuyita, si ekifo ky'okutereka.
- **Etteeka:** FATF Typology — Layering mu bibiina bya financial inclusion.

### 4. Okuŋŋaana kw'Obutunzi kwa Agent (Agent-Banking Concentration) — R10

Agent omu w'obugagga bwa simu (MTN MoMo oba Airtel) abeera n'ebikumi 80%+ by'obutunzi bw'omupolisi mu bbanga:

- **Ekyokulabirako:** Agent omu wa MTN MoMo ateekeddemu UGX 21,000,000 mu bikolwa 6 mu sabbiiti; ebikolwa bino bikozesa 88% y'obutunzi bwonna bw'omupolisi mu bbanga lino.
- **Okugalabika:** Agent emu ayetegeeza ekitundu kinene ky'obutunzi bw'omupolisi.
- **Omukwano:** Ng'agent-banking enkolagana eza Uganda yateekawo, ebikolwa eby'obuŋŋaana bisaba okukebera kw'omukungu.

### 5. Okuddamu Okukozesa Akawunti Eyali Eriho (Dormant Account Reactivation) — R04

Akawunti eyali eriho emisana 180+ ekyuka n'ebyenfuna eby'omuwendo ogwa waggulu:

- **Okugalabika:** Emisana gy'obutuuzigirizi egisusaamu ebbango elyateekebwa; ensimbi nnyingi mu bbanga etonotono.
- **Etteeka:** AMLA 2013 §12 (obukuubirirwa bw'okukebera ekyenfuna ekisaasiddwa).

### 6. Abakulembeze ab'Ekibiina (PEPs) mu Uganda

Uganda yateekateeka ababalirwa mu bakulembeze ab'ekibiina (PEPs). Bino bisobola:

- **Abalekwa ba Gavumenti:** Abakungu ab'etendekero, Ambassada, n'abakiiko bya gavumenti eby'obuwanguzi.
- **Abakiiko:** Abataka b'Olukiiko lw'Obuganda (NRM, FDC, DP, n'ebitongole by'emisango endala), abakiiko b'obutaka.
- **RDC (Resident District Commissioners):** Abakulembeze ab'obutaka ab'obuseera abaatumibwa gavumenti.
- **Abakiiko b'Obutaka:** Abakiiko b'obutaka ab'obuseera mu bika by'Uganda (LC1–LC5).

Nkooye bino, buli mupolisi ayitibwa PEP asaba:
1. Okumanya okulinganira okw'omuwaggulu (Enhanced Due Diligence — EDD).
2. Obukakafu bw'ensibuko y'ensimbi n'ebikolwa.
3. Okukebera kw'omuwaggulu n'okuwanika eri FIA ng'kiteekebwa.

### 7. Ensimbi z'Okutuuka okuva mu Nsi ez'Obucwezi Obuwaggulu (Cross-Border) — R06

Uganda egatta ku nsi omukutu n'ensi z'obuŋŋaana bw'obucwezi obuwaggulu (FATF watchlist). Ebyenfuna ebivaamu:

- **DRC (Kongo y'Obulamogi):** Omukutu omunene gw'ebyenfuna mu Uganda ewaggulu.
- **Somalia:** Ebyenfuna ebivaayo ebisusaamu eddiini ey'okuggya ensimbi z'obusaasi.
- **Nsi ez'olutwe:** Ebibiina n'ensi ezilaagiddwa FATF ne FinCEN.

## Omukwano: Okukebera kw'Mukolisi Omukungu

Buli kigambo ky'obukakafu kitegeeza okukebera kw'omuntu, si kutijja:

1. **Kola okunoonyereza kw'omu nju** okuziba ensibuko y'ensimbi n'ebikolwa.
2. **Ebyenfuna by'ebirabika** bigumizibwa n'omukungu w'amateeka.
3. **Okuwanika eri FIA** kiteekebwa ng'ebirabika bisalibwamu kulaga obucwezi obwa waggulu.
4. **Mwanzi gw'obukakafu** — FIA esobola okulabila omukungu mu bikolwa by'okuwanika.
